01

Discount Guardrail System

Problem: Field reps offer up to 80% discounts without clearance, erasing $156k in profits.

Action: Implement an ERP hard stop capping standard discounts at 15%. Require VP approval for discounts > 20%.

Projected ROI: +$78,000 bottom-line profit recovery (+27.2%).
02

Product Rationalization

Problem: Tables (-$17.7k) and Bookcases (-$3.5k) lose money on every unit sold.

Action: Discontinue bleeding SKUs (Cubify 3D, Chromcraft tables); bundle furniture with high-margin copiers (37% margin).

Projected ROI: Stops -$22,400 in ongoing operational drain.
03

Regional Freight Surcharges

Problem: Central region margin lags at 7.9% due to Texas (-$25.7k) and Illinois shipping deficits.

Action: Introduce localized freight surcharges for heavy items; replicate West region supply-chain efficiencies.

Projected ROI: Lifts Central regional margin to 12.0% (+$20,500 profit).
04

Corporate B2B Account Focus

Problem: Consumer segment exhibits low loyalty and high discount sensitivity.

Action: Pivot marketing budget toward Corporate accounts (AOV $470+) and auto-replenishment paper/toner contracts.

Projected ROI: +10% Corporate volume adds +$71,000 revenue at 17% net margin.
